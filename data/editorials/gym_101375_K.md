Any finite, impartial game under normal play convention is equivalent to a one-pile game of Nim with pile size equal to its Grundy value $g(u) = \text{mex}(\{g(v) : u \to v\})$. A compound game of independent subgames is winning for the first player if and only if $\bigoplus_{i} g_i \ne 0$.
