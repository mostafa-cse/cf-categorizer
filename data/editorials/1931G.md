## 1. Problem Essence & Observation

The problem asks us to determine the optimal answer under the constraints:
- **Time Limit:** `4 seconds`
- **Memory Limit:** `256 megabytes`

### The Core Insight ("Aha!" Moment):
Combinatorial selection $\binom{N}{K} = \frac{N!}{K!(N-K)!} \pmod P$ can be evaluated in $O(1)$ by precomputing factorials $fact[i] = fact[i-1] \cdot i \pmod P$ and inverse factorials $invFact[i] = invFact[i+1] \cdot (i+1) \pmod P$ using Fermat's Little Theorem $a^{P-2} \equiv a^{-1} \pmod P$.

## 2. Algorithmic Paradigm: 310. Large Factorials and Binomial Coefficients

### Why this technique?
Direct division modulo $P$ requires inverse modular exponentiation which takes $O(\log P)$ per query. Precomputing inverse factorials enables $O(1)$ response time.
