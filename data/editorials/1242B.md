## 1. Problem Essence & Observation

The problem asks us to determine the optimal answer under the constraints:
- **Time Limit:** `1 second`
- **Memory Limit:** `256 megabytes`

### The Core Insight ("Aha!" Moment):
Connectivity is an equivalence relation. By organizing connected components into trees and merging smaller trees into larger ones (`union-by-rank/size`) combined with path compression, find and union operations run in near-constant inverse Ackermann time $O(\alpha(N))$.

---

## 2. Algorithmic Paradigm: 80. DSU / Union Find

### Why this technique?
DSU dynamically maintains connected components and cycle detection without running a full DFS/BFS per edge addition.
